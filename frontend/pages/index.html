Why is 1/2 + 1/3 not equal to 2/5? My classmate said we can just add the numerators and denominators directly!

Great question! That is one of the most common misconceptions in mathematics (adding across denominators). Remember: fractions represent portions of different slice sizes. 1/2 slice is much larger than 1/3 slice, so you can't add them directly without finding a common denominator (6ths) first!
