How EduSetu Works

Four evidence-backed steps bridging the gap from conceptual doubt to proven mastery.

1

Ask a Doubt

Type or dictate your confusion in Hindi or English. No question is too basic, and you receive instant patient attention.

2

AI Diagnoses the Gap

Rather than just giving away the final answer, our diagnostic system identifies the exact misconception causing confusion.

3

Learn at Your Pace

Receive step-by-step Socratic explanations with localized analogies, interactive diagrams, and confidence checks.

4

Master & Retain

Track your real-time mastery growth on your concept graph and demonstrate proven retention with diagnostic quizzes.
